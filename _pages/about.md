I am a PhD fellow in Economics at **UNU-MERIT / Maastricht University**. My research sits at the intersection of **gender economics**, **public economics and social policy**, and **development economics**, with a focus on how social norms, institutions and public policy shape women’s labour market outcomes and the allocation of paid and unpaid work within households.

In my work, I combine quantitative and qualitative methods, using both primary and secondary data, driven by a concern with how formal and informal institutions and power structures constrain or enable women's emancipation.

My current projects study:

- **Gender norms and female labour force participation** across European countries.
- **Parental leave reforms**, intrahousehold care dynamics and children's educational choices.
- **Social norms, job attributes and the marriage market in Jordan**, financed by the International Growth Centre.
- **Militarisation and gender inequality**, using evidence from Ecuador's internal armed conflict.

I am a member of UNU-MERIT's Development Economics and Social Protection research groups, and the Dutch Development Economics network. Alongside the PhD, I often work as an external researcher with the European University Institute and I contribute to UNICEF Evaluation Office's Youth Advisory Group.
